# Nano-Influencer Outreach Kit (Product Exchange)

Last updated: 2026-03-20  
Owner: Growth

## Goal
Secure the first 5 nano-influencer collaborations for SmartPrintAI product exchange campaigns.

Target profile:
- Audience size: 5k-100k followers
- Niche fit: custom merch, streetwear, lifestyle, gifts, creator economy, design
- Geography priority: US + Swiss/Europe English-speaking audiences
- Engagement baseline: >= 2.5% average engagement (likes + comments / followers)

## Offer structure (starter)
- Free custom product (hoodie, cap, mug, or phone accessory)
- Optional affiliate code:
  - 10% discount for audience
  - 10%-15% commission for creator (if affiliate system available)
- Deliverables:
  - 1 short-form video or 1 post + 1 story
  - Tag SmartPrintAI
  - Mention creation flow (`smartprintai.com/create`)

## Outreach sequence

1. Discovery (daily)
- Find 10-20 creators matching niche + region.
- Record each lead in tracker CSV.

2. Qualification
- Check recent 10 posts for:
  - content fit,
  - real engagement quality,
  - brand safety.
- Prioritize creators with product-review style content.

3. First message
- Send DM or email using templates below.
- Personalize first line with one specific recent post reference.

4. Follow-up cadence
- Follow-up #1 after 3 days.
- Follow-up #2 after 7 days.
- Close as `no_response` after 14 days.

5. Conversion to active collaboration
- Confirm product choice, size/color, shipping country.
- Send unique link/code and expected posting window.

## DM template (Instagram/TikTok)

Subject equivalent: short opening in first line.

```
Hey <creator_name> — loved your recent <specific_post_topic>.

I run SmartPrintAI, where people generate custom designs and print them on premium products.
We’re opening a small creator exchange program and I’d love to send you a free custom item if you’re open to trying it.

No hard script — just your honest style.
If interested, I’ll share quick details + product options.
```

## Follow-up DM (3 days)

```
Quick follow-up in case this got buried.

Happy to send a free custom piece (hoodie/cap/mug) and a creator code for your audience if useful.
Want me to send the 3-line brief?
```

## Email template

Subject options:
- `Collab idea: free custom merch for <creator_name>`
- `<creator_name> x SmartPrintAI product exchange`

Body:

```
Hi <creator_name>,

I’m reaching out from SmartPrintAI. We help creators and customers turn prompts into custom print-ready designs, then ship the final product.

I’d love to offer you a free custom product in exchange for a simple content collaboration (1 post/video + tag + honest review).

If this is relevant, I can send:
- product options,
- timeline,
- optional audience discount code.

Thanks,
<your_name>
SmartPrintAI
https://smartprintai.com/create
```
